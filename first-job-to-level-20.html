Part 3: First job to level 20 (Magician)

Part 3 of the leveling guide. Source-backed draft; not a measured minimum-time route.

About this guide

Status: source-backed draft, not a measured fastest route. It starts where Part 2 ends: a new level-10 Magician in the Magic Library. It ends in Henesys at level 20.

Ellinia and Henesys have six quests open between levels 10 and 16, plus a delivery to Estelle. They are done in two short visits: Wing and Bruce at level 10, then everything else in one loop at level 16.

When no quest is left at your level the guide says so with a "Go grind" step: grind anywhere you can kill quickly without competing for monsters, then continue. Steps marked SCROLL use the Return Scrolls that Wing and Bruce give you instead of walking.

Reading the images: green circles mark the NPC to talk to; blue circles mark the portal to enter. Each image shows your current map. Artwork is from NiaMeowDB; the highlights were added locally.

Route decisions and skips (5)
Walk from Ellinia to Henesys

The walk is seven maps and free. Players report cabs between towns cost 600 to 1,200 mesos, about all the mesos you have at level 10.

Grind to 16 before the second loop

Shane is in Ellinia and Mrs. Ming Ming and Pia are in Henesys. Doing them at 16 lets one loop and two pairs of Return Scrolls cover all five quests.

Kerning City and Perion quests left for Part 4

Both towns are 10 to 16 maps from Henesys. Quest rewards do not shrink with level, so doing them later, together with their higher-level quests, costs no XP and saves trips.

Skipped quests

Card and Omok pieces have no published drop rates, crafting and Estelle's Garnet need crafted items, and the party and jump quests are not hunting quests (player decision).

No set grinding spot

Servers are crowded, so the best place to grind is wherever you can hold a spot alone and kill monsters in a couple of hits. The guide marks each gap with a "Go grind" step.

Limitations
  • Only Victoria Road maps and the quest pages for this route were collected. Kerning City, Perion and dungeon quests are compared only by distance.
  • XP and mesos are tracked as guaranteed minimums: quest rewards plus required kills only. Part 2's minimum (level 10, 0 grind beyond it) is the starting point, so real play is ahead of these numbers.
  • The source pages do not name the turn-in NPC for most of these quests. The guide hands them back to the giver, as their completion dialogue suggests.
  • Return Scroll destinations are assumed to be the town in the scroll's name.
