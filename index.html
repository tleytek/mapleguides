Maple Island — quest-and-grind route

Part 1 of the leveling guide. Source-backed draft; not a measured minimum-time route.

About this guide

Status: source-backed draft, not a measured fastest route. Scope is Maple Island only. Assumes a fresh solo Beginner starting at Roger, ordinary starter equipment, no funded items, party, XP boosts or teleport shortcuts. Job, target level, combat rates and live build remain unspecified. Endpoint is Southperry ready to depart, not a claimed time-to-level result.

Source: NiaMeowDB's Maple Island index. Map pages warn that values are from the second closed test and live-launch comparison is in progress. Confirm quest availability and objectives in your client.

Follow the route in order. Included quests are instructions, not choices. Skipped quests and rejected hunting maps are listed at the end with the reason each was rejected. These decisions are provisional until timed comparisons confirm or change them.

Reading the images: green circles mark the NPC to talk to; blue circles mark the portal to enter for that specific action. Each image shows your current map, not the destination. The two Mushroom Town maps are distinguished as Roger's map and Nina's map. Open this file in a Markdown preview; all images are local and work offline. Artwork is from NiaMeowDB; the instruction highlights were added locally.

    Why: Mai's Training needs level 3. A Split Road has all three training species, so any levelling done here happens on the map where the training is hunted next. Kills made before accepting do not count toward the training.

    Complete when: Letter for Lucas is active and Maria's Letter is in your inventory.

    Why: Maria is on the same map as Mai, and Lucas is in Amherst, which the route visits for Rain anyway. Accepting now carries the delivery on that trip at no extra travel cost.

    Sources: Letter for Lucas

  3. Map, completion and why

    Complete when: Mai's Training is complete and Mai's Final Training is active.

    Why: A Split Road lists 17 Blue Snails, 14 Shrooms and 11 Red Snails. That is more of each species than the three side forests that also have all three. Training here lets you turn in and pick up Final Training before leaving, so the Orange Mushroom kill happens on the field crossing you already need. This cuts the travel from Mai's first visit to Southperry from 7 portal hops to 5. The West Amherst field has more Shrooms and Red Snails but fewer Blue Snails; see Route decisions for that trade-off. Final Training has no level requirement.

    Sources: Mai's Training · Mai's Final Training · A Split Road

  4. Map, completion and why

    Complete when: You arrive on The Field West of Amherst.

    Why: Amherst, where Lucas and Rain are, is reached only through this field.

Route decisions and skips (9)
Pio's Collecting Recycled Goods is included to pay the ship fare

The ship costs 300 mesos. Of all island quests, only Mai's Final Training (150) and Pio (150) pay mesos, so Pio is the only guaranteed way to the fare without a shop detour. Monster meso drops and sale prices are not established, so skipping Pio cannot be shown to pay the fare. The cost is breaking Amherst's wooden boxes for 2 screws and 2 boards, with unknown drop chances and competition from other players. The reward is 100 quest XP, 150 mesos and a healing chair. This replaces the earlier skip, which did not account for the fare.

Leave at level 7; do not grind to level 8 on the island

Using MeowDB's XP table and each monster's listed EXP, the quest rewards and required kills alone take you to level 7 with about 134 XP left to level 8 (1,131 of 1,265). Level 7 is the ship's stated requirement, so no extra grinding is needed to leave. The last 134 XP is about 9 Orange Mushrooms or 17 Red Snails at listed EXP, and the extra kills on the way usually cover part of it. Grinding here only helps if island monsters give more XP per minute than the first Victoria Island maps, which cannot be checked until that data is collected. A third-party walkthrough also reports Shanks granting 700 EXP on departure, which would put you at level 8 on arrival; this is unverified, so the route does not rely on it.

Mai's Training is hunted on A Split Road, not on The Field West of Amherst

Source counts on A Split Road: 17 Blue Snails (40.5%), 14 Shrooms (33.3%) and 11 Red Snails (26.2%). On The Field West of Amherst: 13 Blue Snails (19.7%), 17 Shrooms (25.8%) and 24 Red Snails (36.4%). Neither map has more of all three species. Training on A Split Road lets you turn in and accept Final Training before leaving. The travel from Mai's first visit to Southperry is then Split Road, field, Amherst, field, Split Road, Southperry: 5 portal hops. Field training forces 7, because Final Training can only be accepted from Mai after the training is turned in. Field training wins only if its larger Shroom and Red Snail supply saves more hunting time than two extra field crossings cost. Neither time is measured, so this is the best found under equal-rate assumptions, not a proven minimum.

The Orange Mushroom is hunted on the field crossing, not in Dangerous Forest

Dangerous Forest lists 12 Orange Mushrooms (35.3% of its monsters), against 5 (7.6%) on The Field West of Amherst. It is a side map of the field, so using it adds two portal hops (in and back out). Only one kill is needed. The forest wins only if finding one Orange Mushroom on the field takes longer than the round trip plus the search inside the forest. Neither time is measured, so the break-even is unknown. The route uses the field because it needs no extra travel, not because it is proven faster.

Skip the other side forests on the West Amherst field

In a Small Forest, Hunting Ground Middle of the Forest I and Hunting Ground Middle of the Forest II each list fewer Blue Snails, Shrooms and Red Snails than A Split Road, and each costs two extra hops. Snail Garden lists more Blue Snails (19) and Mushroom Garden more Shrooms (23), but each lacks at least one training species. Using either means hunting on several maps plus a round trip. No source-count reason justifies these detours.

Skip Biggs's Collection of Items

Biggs (Southperry, level 3) asks for 10 Blue Snail Shells and 1 Orange Mushroom Cap. These are ordinary-item hand-ins, not kill counters. Mai's training kills 10 Blue Snails and Final Training only 1 Orange Mushroom, and the drop rates are unknown, so the route as written does not ensure you hold these items. A guaranteed cap would mean hunting extra Orange Mushrooms for an unknown number of kills. It is also unresolved whether items collected before accepting Biggs's quest count when you first accept it. An early pickup trip should not be assumed necessary, and the route does not schedule one. The quest lists 120 XP and a random Fruit Knife or Razor. Reconsider after testing in a compatible client: carry the drops to Southperry, accept, and try to turn in immediately. Guaranteed XP already reaches level 7 for the ship without Biggs, and Biggs pays no mesos, so skipping him does not block departure.

Skip Heena and Sera's mirror chain

Borrowing Sera's Mirror and Bringing a Mirror to Heena give 2 listed quest XP combined. They need a round trip through the other exit from Roger's map (2 portal hops) plus four interactions. Skipping them is decided by the very small reward; the exact time cost is unmeasured.

Skip shopping and Amherst's eastern branch

No included quest needs a purchase, and no included quest is east of Amherst. Neither detour has a measured benefit. A combat or supply blocker would require revising the route, not silently claiming the detour is efficient.

Mainland quests shown under island monsters are excluded

A matching monster does not make a mainland quest available on Maple Island.

Limitations
  • All 25 maps in the island index were collected, and the source portal data reaches all 25 from Roger's map. The prescribed route takes 13 portal hops from Roger's map to Southperry, then the one-way ship to Lith Harbor; this count excludes walking within maps. Internal identifiers stay in the database and source manifests, not in player instructions or image labels.
  • Source monster counts are spawn-list supply figures (counts, wave spawn counts, total mob count and spawn records agree on every monster map). They are not verified live populations. Respawn behaviour and class-specific reach or kill speed on each map are deliberately not modelled yet. Map choices assume equal kill rates and layouts, so they are a ranking, not a timing.
  • The route is replayed against the retained source data. The check covers quest prerequisites, level and job gates, giver and turn-in locations, kill credit only while a quest is active, each directed portal, and the ship's level, fare and no-active-quests rule. XP and mesos are tracked as guaranteed minimums: quest rewards plus the required kills only, using MeowDB's XP table and monster EXP. Extra kills, drops and meso drops are not counted. On that basis you are level 7 when you board, about 134 XP short of level 8, with exactly the 300-meso fare.
  • Unknown: travel, dialogue and kill times, drop rates, whether item hand-ins accept items collected before the quest, global kill-credit rules, and whether this second-closed-test data matches the live build. No measured minimum time, global optimum or fixed completion time is claimed.
