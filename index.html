MapleGuides: MapleStory Classic leveling route

Follow the parts in order. Your ticked steps are saved in this browser only.

  1. Maple Island — quest-and-grind routeScope is Maple Island only. Assumes a fresh solo Beginner starting at Roger, ordinary starter equipment, no funded items, party, XP boosts or teleport shortcuts. Job, target level, combat rates and live build remain unspecified. Endpoint is Southperry ready to depart, not a claimed time-to-level result.
  2. Part 2: Lith Harbor to first job (Magician)It starts where Part 1 ends: in Lith Harbor, fresh off the ship, about level 8 (7 at minimum) with the mesos you had left after the fare. It ends when you become a Magician in Ellinia at level 10.
